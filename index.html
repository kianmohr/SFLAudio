SFL Audio & Lighting is an Adelaide-based provider of professional audio, lighting and audiovisual solutions.

From powerful nightclub sound systems and multi-zone audio distribution to intelligent lighting control and complete AV integration, we bring technology together to create reliable, high-performance environments.

How it works

From concept
to completion.

Your vision. Our technical expertise.

  1. Consultation

    We start by understanding your space, requirements and what you want to achieve. Whether it's a new installation or an upgrade, we identify the challenges and opportunities.

  2. System Design

    We develop a tailored solution, considering equipment selection, system layout, signal distribution, control requirements and future expandability.

  3. Proposal & Quotation

    You'll receive a clear proposal outlining the recommended solution, project scope and estimated costs.

  4. Installation & Integration

    We bring the system together through installation, equipment configuration, signal routing and integration with your existing infrastructure.

  5. Testing & Optimisation

    We configure and test the system, fine-tuning its performance and ensuring the controls and functionality meet your operational requirements.

  6. Handover & Support

    We explain how to operate your new system and provide guidance on maintaining reliable performance.
